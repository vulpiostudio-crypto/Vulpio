FAQ

Common questions

How long does a project take?+
Landing pages typically take 3–5 days. Full websites take 10–14 days. Web apps vary depending on complexity. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Do I need to provide content and images?+
Ideally yes — you know your brand best. But I can help with placeholder content and stock imagery to get started, and we can refine from there.
Will my site work on mobile?+
Absolutely. Every project I build is mobile-first. I test across all major screen sizes and browsers before handing anything over.
What happens after launch?+
I offer post-launch support to catch any issues. For ongoing maintenance, updates, or new features, we can arrange a support plan.
Can I request changes during the build?+
Yes — within the revision rounds included in your plan. I'll always let you know if a request falls outside scope so there are no surprises.
